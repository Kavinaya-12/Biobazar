BioBazaar Seller Agreement

Version 2026-01. This draft describes the intended marketplace relationship for review. It is not a substitute for jurisdiction-specific legal advice or a signed production agreement.

Application and approval

Submitting an application does not create seller privileges. You may publish through BioBazaar seller tools only after an authorized administrator approves your application. BioBazaar may request additional information, reject an incomplete application, or suspend seller access with a recorded review reason.

Seller responsibilities

You are responsible for the accuracy of your identity and store details, the legality and safety of your products, truthful product descriptions and evidence, inventory accuracy, and complying with applicable product, consumer-protection, tax, privacy, and shipping requirements. Do not claim certifications or environmental benefits unless supported by reliable evidence.

Customer information and fulfilment

Customer shipping information may be used only for fulfilment and related lawful support. Protect it from unauthorized access, do not reuse it for unrelated marketing, and retain it only as required by law and the final marketplace policies.

Commerce and payments

Online checkout, order acceptance, commissions, refunds, seller settlements, and payouts are not enabled in the current application. Do not accept payment or represent an order as placed through BioBazaar until those systems and their separate production terms are activated.

Review and suspension

BioBazaar may pause seller publishing while reviewing a safety, legal, verification, or policy concern. Suspension does not erase historical product, order, payment, refund, or accounting records. Any production process for pending customer orders and disputes must be documented before commerce activation.

Production readiness

This draft must be reviewed and replaced with approved terms that specify the operating jurisdiction, fees, taxes, shipping, cancellation, refund, dispute, privacy, data-retention, termination, and appeal processes before seller applications are accepted in a live marketplace.
